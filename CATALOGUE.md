# Teaching Resources Catalogue

**Source:** `Teaching Resources/` — a primary school teacher's curated collection (~6,300+ files across 70+ top-level folders).

**Context:** Built per the CA3 Final Project brief (Agentic Organisation for teacher resource curation). This catalogue maps all content to key curriculum and functional categories, serving as the taxonomy backbone for the agent pipeline (Researcher → Designer → Maker → Communicator → Manager).

## 1. LITERACY & LANGUAGE (~1,800 files)

### 1.1 Phonics (563 files, 36 subdirs)
Core phonics instruction: CVC, digraphs, blends, bossy r, magic e, floss rule, schwa, vowel digraphs, silent letters, word sorts, decodable comics (6 sets, AUS-UK/USA editions), UFLI readers theatre, Elkonin boxes, fluency lines, phonemic awareness charts/chants, cup-stacking CVC games, tic-tac-toe games, phonics mats, sound boards, label-the-picture activities, corgi blend, beginning sound games, -le syllable, all/oll/ull patterns, ch/tch rules, ce/ge, dge/ge, ng/nk, kn, y as i/e, compound words, alphabet/letter recognition.

### 1.2 Morphology (252 files, 15 subdirs)
Structured morphology programme: 27-week plan (3rd–6th class) covering re-, struct, scrib/script, spec/spect/spic, tract, er/or, trans, bio, form, ject, port, rupt, vis/vid, con, phon, able/ible, graph/inter, dic/dict, cred, assimilated prefixes, auto, tri, pos/pon/pound, mit/miss, cur/curs, duc, fac/fact/fect/fic. Supporting: affixes (prefixes, suffix bundle), matrices, word ladders (Greek & Latin bases), endings, adding -ing, cloze passages, roll-and-read dice games, pick-up games, word mats, "Teach Along with Me" course, Treasa Bowe Effective Spelling Course (Modules 2 & 4).

### 1.3 Spelling (34 files, 2 subdirs)
